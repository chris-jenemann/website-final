Mount Edith

Townsend, MT 4/26

Chris Jenemann 6/8/26

Goats and Snow

The Helena crew and I were home for Thanksgiving weekend when a surprise snow storm would hit Montana and firing us up to get out in the backcountry. We turned our sights on Mount Edith in the Big Belt Mountains, near Townsend. This is not common objective as it is inaccessible for most of the winter season, but we were able to make within a mile or two of the trailhead no issue with about a foot of snow on the ground at 6000 feet. We skinned up the standard Mount Edith trail and found less snow then we were hoping for though still enough to make due. After ditching our skis we topped out on the false summit of the mountain, not seeing a reason to continue up the windblown ridge to the true top.

On our way back to our skis we were lucky enough to run into the Big Belt Herd of mountain goats. This was by far the most I had ever seen in one place with what seemed like a solid 25-30 sheep all huddled together on the ridge. This movement lead to some of the coolest images i’ve captured.

The herd of sheep above the snowy Missouri River valley and the Bridgers and Elkhorns encapsulates the beauty of Montana in one scene.

After the sheep moved on we transitioned for downhill and made our way down the trail we came up careful to avoid any of the branches sticking out of the shallow snowpack.

The skiing was just ok with alot of it either being too crusty or shallow to have much fun. We all still left feeling lucky to have witnessed a once-in-a-lifetime moment in the mountains. Below is a GPS video and you can the Strava here.
