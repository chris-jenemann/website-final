Backcountry Ski

Mt.Brown

Glacier, MT 2/26

Chris Jenemann 6/4/26

Escaping the post climate disaster rains of Missoula for the rains of Glacier...

It felt more like spring than mid-winter in Missoula this past January. Regardless, my buddies Fox Rowland and Eli West were still up for a weekend ski adventure in Glacier. We left campus early Saturday morning, stopping quickly in Polson to buy the ingredients for dinner, before booking it the rest of the way to the park. We arrived around 10:30 and started the hike in search of snow.

After about 3 miles and 2300 feet of gain, we skinned up. As in much of Montana, Glacier was covered in deadfall from numerous windstorms, though once on the skis, the going got quicker and more enjoyable.

After about another 1000 feet of climbing, we went into the clouds. It finally started to feel more like winter, snow ghosts, trees caked in hoarfrost, rose out of the rapidly deepening snowpack.

Once above the clouds, Lake McDonald gleamed below us as the surrounding mountains faded in and out of view as the clouds flowed over the landscape.

We pushed up the ridge to the lookout. At this point, the snowpack was stout, with drifts reaching up towards the observation deck of the fire watch, and still deeper than our probes in more protected areas.

From the tower, we enjoyed views of Stanton Mountain, Mount Brown, and the Little Matterhorn. Caked in rime and clouds, these peaks seem straight off the cover of Alpinist. It’s incredible how winter can transform one of the most accessible lookouts in the park into what feels like a true big mountain destination.

After a long lunch, we skinned back across the ride before descending to our trail runners. The skiing was nothing special besides the spectacular view.

This route is pretty incredible, and with its mellow terrain, it's definitely on the list to come back to on a better snow year. Every time I get a chance to visit Glacier, I'm wowed by its beauty and deeply grateful to call Montana home.

After dealing with the deadfall and making the short slog back to the car, we snapped a celebratory pic and set up camp at Apgar. After a quick dinner of pasta and honey stingers, we crawled into our tent and knocked out. Below is a GPS video of the ski, and you can find the Strava here.
